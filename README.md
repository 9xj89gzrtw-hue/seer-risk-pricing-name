# Seer — naming proposal for "Risk Pricing"

`risk.seer.pm` shows **market estimates of risk across DeFi assets**, so someone sizing their
exposure can compare those estimates instead of reading a single yes-or-no price in isolation.
That product needs a name.

Three proposals, one per poidh claim (bounty #39). Each card is a simple square image of the name.

| | name | card | the idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Contour** | [`contour.png`](contour.png) | risk is a field, not a point |
| 2 | **Plumb** | [`plumb.png`](plumb.png) | measure the true depth, exactly |
| 3 | **Gauge** | [`gauge.png`](gauge.png) | one comparable number per protocol |

## Contour

A contour line is the set of all points that share one value. So a contour is literally *a
price* of something, drawn on a field. DeFi risk is exactly that kind of field: every protocol
is a point, and the level through those points is what you actually want to read. Seer Contour
gives every protocol a level on the same scale, so you can compare exposures across the stack
instead of squinting at one protocol's yes/no price in isolation. It also sits next to the rest
of the family — Deep, Futarchy, Opportunity Markets — as a plain noun for a way of seeing.

## Plumb

To plumb is to sound a depth exactly, and "plumb" on its own also means *precisely right*.
Risk pricing is an exercise in sounding depth: Plumb gives each protocol's market-implied
estimate as a number you can put next to the rest of your book, so you can size total exposure
rather than judge one venue at a time. It is short, it is ownable, and it says *measured*, not
modelled.

## Gauge

A gauge tells you your level without a dashboard of warnings. Seer Gauge turns each protocol's
market-implied risk into one comparable number, so you can see where you are across the whole
stack at a glance, rather than reading a separate verdict per protocol.
